Warranty & Safety Terms

Please review our warranty conditions and safety guidelines to ensure optimal performance.

❌ Warranty Void If:

  • Seal Broken or Removed
  • Battery Opened or Tampered
  • Water / Moisture Damage
  • Physical Damage or Mishandling
  • Wrong Charger Used
  • Reverse Polarity
  • Short Circuit
  • Serial No. Altered or Removed

⚠️ Important Warnings:

  • Use Recommended Charger Only
  • Keep Away from Water & Fire
  • Do Not Open Battery Pack
  • Do Not Short Circuit Terminals
  • Do Not Drop, Crush, or Puncture
  • Stop Use if Swollen, Overheated, or Damaged
